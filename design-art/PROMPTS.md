## Shared art direction

Use case: ui-mockup. Deliverable: ONE finished, high-resolution desktop website page mockup, flat front-on UI artwork, landscape 16:10 composition. This is visual concept art only, not code. Fill the image with the actual page; no laptop frame, no perspective, no presentation-board margins, no multi-screen collage.
REFERENCE ROLES: The attached DeepStem HUB flyers and website crops are brand STYLE REFERENCES; use their real logo, navy/green/white palette and distinctive bold condensed, squared sans-serif heading style. The old game-jam screenshots are WORKFLOW REFERENCES only; redesign rather than copy their dark purple look.
BRAND INVARIANTS: faithfully reproduce the DeepStem HUB mark from the flyer: one small green horizontal organic wave at the top, descending navy organic wave rows narrowing toward a navy round dot; green wordmark spelled exactly "DeepStem HUB". Do not substitute a gamepad logo. Navy #08265B; vivid green #2DBD66; clean white #FFFFFF; very pale mint #F0F8F4; restrained teal #00AFA9. Headings resemble the bold, slightly condensed, squared geometric type in the "Our Focus Areas" website crop; body is a clean readable humanist sans serif. No purple accents.
ART DIRECTION: a confident, friendly creative studio for first-year university students in Ghana. Strong editorial hierarchy, large comfortably legible interface type, roomy controls, balanced purposeful spacing. White content surfaces with deep navy structural areas and green primary buttons. Fine navy outlines, soft restrained shadows, 12–16 px corner radii. Signature organic wave/dot brand motifs used sparingly at the bottom edge or in a faint illustration backdrop. Realistic, coherent, buildable UI, never a dense enterprise dashboard. Polished pixel-game artwork inside game thumbnails only. No tiny filler paragraphs, no fake-code texture, no random decorative charts, no contradictory status messages. All visible UI text must be clean, correctly spelled and easy to read. No watermark.
